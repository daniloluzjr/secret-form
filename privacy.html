Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • InisCare Healthcare • Ireland

Overview: InisCare is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of personal and special category health data processed through this Supervisors Documentation Portal and associated mobile applications, in strict compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679 and the Irish Data Protection Acts 1988–2018.

1. Data Controller

InisCare operates as the Data Controller in respect of home care documentation, staff evaluations, and supervisory records. For data protection inquiries, you may reach our compliance team via our Support & Contact page or by emailing info@iniscare.ie.

2. Information We Process

This portal is an operational documentation tool used exclusively by authorized healthcare supervisors and assessors. Depending on the form utilized, the following categories of data may be processed:

  • Healthcare Assistant & Staff Information: Full name, staff identifier, contact telephone, training verification, supervisory evaluation notes, and digital signatures.
  • Service User (Customer) Information: Name, address, care requirements, Activities of Daily Living (ADL) assessments, and emergency contact details necessary for safe care delivery.
  • Special Category Health Data: Medical history, medication support plans, falls risk scores, mobility assessments, and clinical incident reports.
  • Technical & Audit Logs: Timestamp of form submission, document ID, and verification hashes.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

Data collected via this application is processed under:

  • Article 6(1)(b) & (c) GDPR: Performance of home care service contracts and compliance with statutory healthcare obligations in Ireland (e.g., Health Act 2007, HIQA standards).
  • Article 9(2)(h) GDPR: Management of health or social care systems and services, provision of health care, and clinical governance.

4. Data Retention & Auto-Purging Policy

In accordance with the principle of storage limitation (data minimization):

  • Temporary PDF download links and digital audit logs generated through this portal are subject to an automated 45-day retention policy, after which temporary storage files are permanently purged.
  • Final signed records delivered to the central InisCare care management repository are archived in accordance with statutory medical and health record retention schedules mandated by Irish law and HSE service level agreements.

5. Confidentiality & Third-Party Sharing

Access to documentation is strictly role-based and restricted to authorized InisCare supervisory, clinical governance, and quality assurance personnel. We do not sell, rent, or commercialize any personal or health data. Data is only disclosed to external statutory bodies (such as the HSE Safeguarding Team, Health and Safety Authority - HSA, or An Garda Síochána) where strictly required by Irish law or mandatory regulatory reporting triggers.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

This portal does not use advertising, profiling, or third-party tracking cookies.

  • Local Draft Storage (Browser / App Storage): We utilize client-side localStorage solely to provide offline auto-save capabilities. This prevents supervisors from losing draft entries if network connectivity drops while conducting home visits. Drafts remain on the user's local device until cleared or submitted.
  • Session Security: Ephemeral session tokens may be used to verify authorized access to the administrative logs gate.

7. Your Rights under GDPR

Under European and Irish data protection law, individuals have the following rights regarding their personal data, subject to statutory healthcare exemptions:

  • The right to access personal data held about you.
  • The right to request rectification of inaccurate data.
  • The right to request erasure or restriction of processing where applicable by law.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission (DPC) of Ireland (www.dataprotection.ie).

8. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please submit an inquiry through our dedicated Support & Contact page.
