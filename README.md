# 📋 InisCare - Supervisors Documentation Portal

Web portal with 4 electronic supervisory forms, interactive digital touch/mouse signatures, pixel-perfect PDF export matching official InisCare documents, and automatic email dispatch with custom target email support.

---

## 📑 Included Forms & Checklists

1. **Healthcare Assistant Spot Check (`spot-check.html`)**
   - 9 supervisory checkpoints (Timekeeping, ID Badge, Hygiene, IPC, Mobile usage, Care plan tasks, Communication, Line manager reporting).
   - Comments section.
   - Supervisor digital signature & date.

2. **Healthcare Assistant Supervision Checklist (`supervision-checklist.html`)**
   - **Landscape (A4) format** matching official document.
   - Comprehensive 2-page supervision evaluation (Exceeds requirements, Meets requirements, Does not meet requirements, Not observed).
   - 13 detailed performance & compliance criteria with individual notes.
   - Dual digital signatures (Care Assistant & Supervisor) with dates.

3. **Care Assistant Shadow Shift (`shadow-shift.html`)**
   - 2-page portrait document.
   - Shift dates and times log.
   - 12 core care tasks evaluation (Observed / Completed).
   - 8 supervisory interaction, IPC, PM&H, and care plan review sections.
   - Supervisor digital signature & date.

4. **Staff Phone & WhatsApp Consent Form (`consent-form.html`)**
   - Official staff consent for phone number storage and WhatsApp group communication.
   - Digital signature & date.

---

## ✉️ Direct Email Dispatch Feature
Each form includes an optional **"Send copy to email"** field located directly above the action button. 
- When filled, a copy of the signed PDF is instantly dispatched to that specific recipient.
- The system also retains the default company notifications for recordkeeping and backup.
