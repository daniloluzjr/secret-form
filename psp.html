8. Housekeeping Scope • Service Agreement Service Standards
InisCare Policy: InisCare staff are professional Healthcare Assistants dedicated to supporting safe independent living, not domestic cleaners. Tasks are light household duties directly supporting customer welfare:

✓ Permitted Light Housekeeping Tasks

  • Hoovering rooms used by the Customer (weekly or as reasonably needed)
  • Washing up the Customer's used dishes only
  • Customer's personal laundry (clothing only)
  • Mopping kitchen/bathroom floors (once a week / as needed)
  • Cleaning bathroom after use (toilet, shower/bath wipe down)
  • Making Customer's bed and changing bedsheets
  • Wiping kitchen countertops after use
  • Emptying household bins
  • Checking fridge for expired food & wiping spills
  • Cleaning fireplace in exceptional cases (no other heat source)
  • Dusting surfaces within easy reach (excluding delicate ornaments)
  • Cleaning microwave after use by carer

✗ Strictly Excluded Tasks

  • Gardening or outdoor maintenance
  • Cleaning ovens or deep cooker scrubbing
  • Cleaning kitchen cupboard doors
  • Window washing
  • Deep cleaning (scrubbing floors, skirting boards, walls)
  • Moving heavy furniture to clean behind
  • Standing on furniture/ladders to reach high cobwebs
  • Painting or home decorating
  • Tidying private wardrobes/drawers of customer personal effects
  • Defrosting freezers
  • Draining or servicing washing machines
