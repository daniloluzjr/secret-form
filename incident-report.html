Data Protection & GDPR Notice

The personal data and special category health data collected on this form are processed by InisCare in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679 and the Data Protection Acts 1988–2018.

  • Purpose: Information is collected solely for incident management, risk mitigation, statutory reporting (including HSE NIMS / HSA), quality assurance, legal compliance, and protecting the health and safety of clients and employees.
  • Confidentiality & Sharing: Access is strictly restricted to authorised quality, clinical, and management personnel. Information will only be disclosed to external statutory bodies (e.g., HSE Safeguarding, Health & Safety Authority, State Claims Agency, An Garda Síochána) where required by law or contractual service agreements.
  • Retention: Forms are retained securely in compliance with InisCare's Data Retention Policy and statutory record-keeping periods for health and social care providers in Ireland.
